About TwinGuard AI

Everything a judge needs to understand the idea in 60 seconds.

🚩 The Problem

Factories lose crores every year to unplanned machine downtime. Most maintenance today is either reactive (fix after it breaks — expensive) or scheduled (fixed intervals — wasteful). Operators rarely know a failure is coming until it's too late.

✅ The Solution

TwinGuard AI builds a live digital twin of every machine from sensor data (temperature, vibration, power, load). A prediction model scores health in real time, explains why risk is rising, and recommends the exact maintenance action — before failure occurs.

⚙ How It Works — Architecture

📡SensorsTemp · Vibration · Power · Load
🧮Digital TwinLive per-machine model
🧠AI PredictionHealth score + failure %
💬ExplainabilityRoot-cause AI assistant
🛠ActionAuto-generated maintenance task

🧰 Tech Stack (Prototype)

HTML5 / CSS3 / vanilla JavaScript · Canvas-based real-time charting · Rule-based predictive scoring engine · Explainable rule-driven chat assistant.

Production roadmap: IoT sensor ingestion (MQTT), time-series DB (InfluxDB), ML model (LSTM/XGBoost for RUL prediction), cloud dashboard, SMS/WhatsApp maintenance alerts.
