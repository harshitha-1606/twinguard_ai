PIPELINE — CAMERA TO PREDICTION

Zero-sensor digital twin, end to end

1

Camera Capture

A standard RGB camera (phone or CCTV feed) streams continuous video of the physical asset. No IoT sensors, no retrofitting — just an existing lens pointed at the machine.

INPUT: RGB VIDEO
2

Computer Vision Processing

Frame-by-frame inference (pose estimation, object detection, optical flow) extracts motion, vibration, temperature-proxy cues, and wear indicators directly from pixels.

MODEL: CNN / VISION TRANSFORMER
3

Feature Extraction & Tracking

Key points on the asset (shaft rotation, disc alignment, body sway) are tracked across frames and converted into structured time-series signals.

OUTPUT: TIME-SERIES FEATURES
4

Digital Twin Synchronization

Extracted features stream in real time to a lightweight 3D twin (the orange wireframe model), which mirrors the physical asset's pose, speed, and state continuously.

SYNC: LIVE STATE MIRROR
5

Predictive Modeling

Historical + live signals feed a forecasting model that projects wear trends forward, comparing current behavior against learned failure signatures.

MODEL: TIME-SERIES FORECASTING
6

Anomaly Detection & Alert

When projected wear crosses a safety threshold, the twin flags a predicted failure window (e.g. 72H) and triggers an alert — before the physical asset actually fails.

OUTPUT: EARLY-WARNING ALERT
